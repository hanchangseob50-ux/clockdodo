Pomodoro Timer · Study Timer for Better Focus

Manage focus and break periods with a Pomodoro timer. Use this free time-management tool for study, work, and personal development.

Open the pomodoro timer

When this tool helps

The Pomodoro method makes focused work and rest visible. ClockDodo starts with 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of rest, and lets you change both lengths.

Exercise and movement

Use a short focus block for mobility practice or a home exercise circuit, then take a break for water and recovery. For precise repeating athletic intervals, choose the interval timer.

Study

Set a 50-minute class or study block followed by a 10-minute break. Adjust the focus and break lengths to fit your schedule.

Work

Set a focus session for writing, design review, planning, or one administrative task. A defined block can help protect time from email and chat interruptions.

Everyday life

Apply a short session to decluttering one shelf, practicing an instrument, meal prep, or personal paperwork. Smaller rounds can make it easier to begin and stop.

How to use it

  1. Choose focus and break lengths that fit the task.
  2. Start a focus session and work on one defined activity.
  3. Take the break when it begins, then continue or reset as needed.

Common questions

Do I have to use 25 minutes and 5 minutes?

No. Those are the starting values. Adjust focus and break lengths for the activity and your routine.

Is Pomodoro the same as an interval timer?

Both alternate periods, but Pomodoro is designed around focus and breaks. The interval timer is better for exercise or custom work-rest rounds.
