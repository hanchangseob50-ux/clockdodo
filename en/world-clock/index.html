World Clock for Real Life

Compare the current local time in several cities at a glance. Add cities you need and keep their clocks together while planning across locations.

Open the world clock

When this tool helps

A world clock is useful when the same moment happens at different local times. Compare cities before scheduling, then use the converter for a specific future date and time.

Exercise and sports

Find a convenient time for a virtual training group across countries or check when an international race stream starts locally. Confirm event schedules with the organizer.

Study

Compare your time with a remote study partner, online class, or webinar host. Check the city clock before joining.

Work

Keep client, teammate, and support locations visible during a distributed workday. This helps avoid proposing a meeting during someone else?s night.

Everyday life

Check the time before calling family abroad, planning travel check-ins, or watching a live broadcast. Add cities you regularly need.

How to use it

  1. Open World Clock from the tool list.
  2. Review the included cities and add another from the selector.
  3. Compare the displayed times before arranging a call or event.

Common questions

Does the world clock show future meeting times?

It shows current city times. Use the Time Zone Converter to convert a specific future date and time.

Can I save a city list?

ClockDodo saves added cities in this browser when storage is available.
