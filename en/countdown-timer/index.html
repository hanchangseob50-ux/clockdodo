Countdown Timer for Real Life

Start a duration-based countdown for a task, practice round, or break. Choose minutes and seconds, pause, reset, or reuse a preset.

Open the countdown timer

When this tool helps

A countdown answers ?how much time is left?? Set a duration, start it, and use the remaining time as a simple boundary for one activity.

Exercise

Try a 20-minute easy morning run, or time a 5-minute warm-up followed by 20 minutes of exercise. Adjust the example to your fitness and schedule.

Study

Give a practice quiz 20 minutes, read one chapter for 15 minutes, or use a 25-minute focus block before taking a break.

Work

Set a 10-minute limit for inbox triage, a 30-minute drafting sprint, or a reminder to wrap up before a meeting.

Everyday life

Time a laundry check, tea steep, cooking step, tidy-up sprint, or screen break. A browser timer is a reminder, not a substitute for checking appliances or food safely.

How to use it

  1. Enter minutes and seconds, or choose a preset.
  2. Select Start and keep the page open while the countdown runs.
  3. Pause when needed; reset before starting a different task.

Common questions

Can I use the countdown after closing the page?

Keep the page open. A browser timer cannot reliably play a sound after its page is closed or the device is asleep.

When should I choose an interval timer?

Choose an interval timer for repeated work and rest periods. A countdown is better for one duration.
