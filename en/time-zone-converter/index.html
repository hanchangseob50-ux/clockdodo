Time Zone Converter for Real Life

Choose a date and time in one supported city and convert it to another. A time zone converter helps when an invitation or event is listed far from your local time.

Open the time zone converter

When this tool helps

Always convert the date as well as the hour. Daylight saving changes and date boundaries can shift the result, especially when one city is many hours ahead of another.

Exercise and sports

Convert an online coaching session or international event start time before adding it to your training plan. Recheck the organizer?s final schedule on event day.

Study

Translate a live lecture, admissions webinar, or remote study group into your city?s date and hour. This is useful when the result lands on the previous or next day.

Work

Find a workable slot for a cross-border client call, interview, product launch, or team handoff. Share both city times in the invitation.

Everyday life

Convert a family call, flight check-in, livestream, or appointment abroad. For travel, check the airline or venue confirmation as the final source.

How to use it

  1. Enter the date and time as it applies in the source city.
  2. Choose the source city and destination city.
  3. Select Convert and check both the resulting time and date.

Common questions

Does the converter account for daylight saving time?

It uses the browser time-zone database for the selected cities. For important events, confirm the final time with the organizer.

Why can the converted date be different?

The destination city may be many hours ahead or behind the source city, so the same instant can fall on the previous or next calendar day.
